## Inversion of Cognitive Architecture as a Paradigm Shift
Historically, both cognitive science as well as Artificial Intelligence have relied on a fundamental "corticocentric" axiom, the assumption that it is the cortex that primarily handles the executive generation of sequence and logic. This computational model challenges this axiom by mathematically demonstrating the following:

1. The Energy Miser: With the addition of a cerebellar predictive network there is a drastic lowering of the gradient norm (neuroplastic energy) that is required by the cortex for the tracking of complex sequences. The cortex functions mostly as a passive moniter for semantics and narritive.
2. Dysmetria of Thought (CCAS): Ablating the cerebellar network forces the cortex to then "manually steer" by using delayed feedback. The simulation used demonstrates that this causes an immediate spike in tracking error (loss), which mimics the clinical presentation of Cerebellar Cognitive Affective Syndrome and the "hallucination" problems within modern usage and development of LLMs.
